Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Reckon Technologies Ltd. ("Reckon", "we", "us") runs Reckon Sales Agent, a sales and marketing tool used by businesses ("Customers") to talk with their leads and customers. This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, and what rights you have.

1. Who this policy covers

It covers people who message, comment on or submit a form to a Customer's Facebook Page, Instagram account or WhatsApp number; people who call or text a Customer's phone number; and people who receive email from a Customer. Reckon acts as a service provider. The Customer decides how the data is used, and this policy describes what we do on its behalf.

2. Data we handle

  • Messages and comments sent to a Customer's Facebook Page or Instagram account, and to its WhatsApp number.
  • Lead form answers from Meta lead ads and LinkedIn lead forms, such as name, email and phone number.
  • Contact details a Customer enters or imports, and the names and profile details that Meta or WhatsApp provide when someone writes in.
  • Call and SMS records from the telephone services a Customer connects, including call times and, where a Customer enables it, recordings and transcripts.
  • Email delivery events from the email provider, such as delivered, opened, bounced or unsubscribed.
  • Public website information the agents read from a lead's own website to research the business.
  • Access tokens that let us act for a Customer's connected Facebook Page, Instagram account, WhatsApp account or other service. These are stored encrypted and used only for that Customer.

3. How we use it

  • To show conversations, leads and prospects to the Customer's own team.
  • To draft replies and posts, and to send them only when the Customer or its approval rules allow.
  • To place lead ad and lead form submissions into the Customer's prospect list.
  • To keep usage records, check that the service is working, and keep it secure.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use one Customer's data for another Customer.

4. AI processing

To draft replies and summaries, the content of a conversation may be sent to an AI model provider chosen by the platform or by the Customer: Anthropic, OpenAI, or a local model run by the Customer. We send only the text needed for the task. The provider's own terms apply to its handling of that text.

5. Data from Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

Data we receive from Meta is used only to provide the features the Customer has connected: messages, comments, lead forms and publishing. It is kept against that Customer's account, and it is deleted when the Customer disconnects the account or asks us to delete it, as described in section 8.

6. Who we share data with

We share data only with service providers that we need to run the platform, and only for that purpose: hosting, Meta, WhatsApp Business Platform, Twilio (calls and SMS), SendGrid and other email providers, and the AI providers in section 4. We may also disclose data where the law requires it.

7. How long we keep it

We keep data for as long as the Customer's account is active, and for a limited period after that for legal and security reasons. Customers can set shorter retention periods for lead search data. When an access token is disconnected, we stop using it straight away and delete it.

8. Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data at any time. If you messaged or submitted a form to one of our Customers and want your data deleted, contact the business you spoke with, or email us at info@reckon.tech. Include the Facebook, Instagram or WhatsApp name or number you used. We will delete the matching data and confirm when we are done.

Data deletion instructions for Meta: send a request to info@reckon.tech with the subject "Data deletion". We confirm each request within 30 days.

9. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Access tokens and secrets are stored encrypted and never shown again after they are saved. Each Customer's data is separated from every other Customer's, and access is limited to people who need it for their work.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when our practices change and show the new date at the top. Material changes will be announced to Customers before they take effect.
