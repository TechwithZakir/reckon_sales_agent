# AI Sales Agent for Frappe CRM: Lead Discovery, Scoring and Approved Follow-Up

Sales teams in Bangladesh spend hours finding prospects, checking their websites and writing follow-ups. **Reckon Sales AI Agent**, the engine behind Reckon SalesPilot from Reckon Technologies Ltd., automates the research and the paperwork while a person approves what gets sent. It is a Python-first agent for Frappe CRM, sold as a module of the Reckon SaaS Platform.

## Detailed features

### Lead discovery

- Discovery campaigns from keywords or industries, locations and a target count, with a preview of the planned searches before anything runs
- Sources include your own CSV lists, a permitted-directory crawler, Brave Search (Place Search, LLM Context and Web Search) and Google Maps providers such as the Google Places API, SerpAPI, Scrapingdog, ScrapingBee and Apify
- Smart Sources runs every active provider, merges the results and keeps each business once
- Scheduled discovery that runs daily, weekly or monthly from a targeting profile
- Lead Finder Studio: one page to pick a source, define who to find, set the schedule and watch live progress

### Market research agent

- Describe what you sell and who buys it, and the agent researches your market from the web
- Produces a market brief with customer segments, search keywords, Bangla variants, locations and buying signals
- Approved segments become targeting profiles ready for discovery

### Website research and enrichment

- Reads up to four pages per site, respecting robots.txt, and extracts emails, phones, social profiles and evidence
- Optional Claude extraction where every fact must quote text that is on the page
- Website finder for listings that have no website
- Buying signals such as hiring, a new branch or a tender, in English and Bangla

### Scoring and prioritising

- Deterministic, versioned lead score covering industry fit, operational complexity, need signal, scale and evidence quality
- Optional AI assessment blended in at a capped share
- Explainable deal probability calibrated on each company's own lead-to-deal history
- Manager override with a recorded reason that survives re-scoring

### Review and CRM sync

- Prospect Review page to approve, reject, defer, merge or re-research in bulk
- Deduplication by source reference, website domain, normalised phone and name plus city
- Approved prospects become a CRM Organization and a CRM Lead with a research note, filling empty fields only

### Omni-channel campaigns

- Multi-step sequences across email, SMS, WhatsApp and call or LinkedIn tasks
- Safe placeholders and optional AI-personalised opening lines
- Write with AI for English or Bangla sequences, using only approved knowledge
- Campaign pilot mode with a hold-out group and a conversion verdict

### Replies and engagement

- Replies classified as interested, meeting request, question, objection, not now, not interested, wrong person, complaint, unsubscribe or out of office, in English and Bangla
- Unsubscribes and complaints stop the sequence and suppress the contact
- Routine questions answered only from the approved Knowledge Library
- Positive replies create a CRM task and a timeline note

### Safety and governance

- Outbound sending is off by default, with dry runs first
- Re-check of kill switch, subscription, suppression list, approval and daily limits before every send
- Approvals bound to the exact content, audience and schedule, voided by any change
- Content check for banned claims, unsupported figures and unknown placeholders
- Consent required for SMS and WhatsApp
- Append-only audit trail naming the agent and policy outcome

### Analytics

- Funnel from discovery to revenue by segment, source and campaign
- Weekly optimisation insights, with AI analysis of aggregated numbers only
- Provider usage counters and monthly budgets

### Social publishing

- Connect your own Facebook Page, Instagram professional account or LinkedIn page
- AI-written captions from approved knowledge, with a person approving the exact post
- Publishing through the official APIs only

### Multi-company SaaS

- Registers as the Sales AI Agent module with plans, metering and quotas
- Strict per-company separation of CRM and agent data

## Built on Frappe CRM

Reckon Sales AI Agent runs on Frappe CRM and keeps Frappe CRM records authoritative. Four agents (Research, Content, Engagement and Analytics) work for each company under a policy engine where approval is a permission boundary, not a confidence threshold.

## Who it is for

B2B sales teams, distributors, manufacturers, software and service companies in Bangladesh and beyond that want a steady flow of researched, scored and approved leads.
